# Nijatva Messages — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Nijatva Messages is a private SMS app for Android.

## Data We Collect

**None.** The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data. There are no analytics, no ads and no trackers, and the app has no internet permission.

## Data On Your Device

Messages are read from and written to Android's own SMS database on your device. The app stores no copy elsewhere. Uninstalling the app does not delete your messages.

## Permissions

- **SMS (read/send/receive)** — required to show conversations and send messages
- **Contacts (read)** — only to show contact names next to phone numbers
- **Notifications** — to alert you about new messages

No location, camera, microphone, storage or internet access.

## Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue on this repository.
